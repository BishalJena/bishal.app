Privacy Policy

Lotus F-1 exists to protect your privacy, so it's built to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what we collect through this website today and what the app is designed to do once it launches.

This website and the waitlist

When you join the waitlist, we store your email address, which app you signed up for, and the time you signed up. We also store your approximate location (country, region, city and time zone, estimated from your IP address by Cloudflare; we don't store the IP address itself) and how you found this site (the page you signed up on, the site that linked you here, and any campaign tags in the link). We use this only to tell you about Lotus F-1's launch and major updates, and to understand where interest in it comes from. We don't sell or share it, and every email includes a way to unsubscribe.

Waitlist sign-ups are stored with our hosting provider, Cloudflare. To remove your email from the waitlist, contact us at hello@lotus-f1.app.

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies or track you across other websites.

Scans (once the app launches)

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and NFC scans run on your iPhone. Scan results stay on your device unless you choose to share a summary. We don't collect your location or which accommodation you're staying in.

Changes to this policy

Lotus F-1 hasn't launched yet. We'll update this policy before launch to reflect exactly how the app works, and note material changes in the release notes.

Questions? Reach us at hello@lotus-f1.app.
